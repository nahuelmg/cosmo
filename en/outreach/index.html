Outreach

We believe in the importance of bringing science closer to society. We participate in talks, workshops, and outreach publications aimed at broad audiences and the educational community.

talk

Gravitational Waves: Listening to the Dark Universe

August 14, 2025

Popular-science lecture at the Galileo Galilei Planetarium in Buenos Aires on the detection of gravitational waves and what they reveal about black holes and neutron stars. The event drew over 300 attendees and was streamed live on the planetarium's YouTube channel.

Learn more →

school-visit

What Is Dark Matter? A School Visit

June 5, 2025

Visit to Colegio Nacional Buenos Aires to talk with fifth and sixth year students about dark matter, computational simulations, and careers in astrophysics. The activity included a workshop on visualising N-body simulations using free software.

article

The Early Universe under the Magnifying Glass: Inflation and the Cosmic Microwave Background

November 20, 2024

Opinion article published in the Science section of La Nacion, explaining to the general public what cosmic inflation is, how the cosmic microwave background acts as a photograph of the baby universe, and what we can learn from its small temperature fluctuations.

Learn more →

interview

Interview on the Ciencia Argentina Podcast

April 3, 2024

45-minute interview on the Ciencia Argentina podcast about the group's work in computational cosmology, the Hubble tension, and the future of multi-messenger astronomy. The episode was downloaded more than 8,000 times in its first week.
