<objective>
Resize and reshape PersonCard so member photos no longer dominate the /people directory, and update the PeopleSection grid so cards cap at ≤240 px on every breakpoint from lg up. Closes MEDIA-01 and the PersonCard half of MEDIA-05.

Purpose: Member photos currently render at ~33vw on lg (~340–640 px) which visually overwhelms the encyclopedic academic directory tone the v1.2 polish is chasing. Capping the card, switching to a 4:5 portrait aspect, and tightening the next/image sizes string makes photos read as quiet supporting context while also cutting srcset bytes downloaded per card.

Output:
- PersonCard caps at max-w-[240px], centers in cell, uses aspect-[4/5] portrait photo
- PeopleSection grid adds xl:grid-cols-4
- next/image sizes string rewritten to `(min-width: 640px) 240px, 100vw`
</objective>
