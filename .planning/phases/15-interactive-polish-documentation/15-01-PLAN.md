    - WRAP the visible dot inside a `<span aria-hidden className="block w-2.5 h-2.5 rounded-full {bg}" />`.
    - Apply `p-[17px]` (17px padding × 2 + 10px dot = 44px) and `flex items-center justify-center` to the `<button>`.
